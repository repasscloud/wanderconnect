## Status vocabulary

CONFIRMED means explicitly requested by the owner in this conversation. DEFAULT means a recommended implementation choice that may be changed. OPEN means a business/provider/policy decision requiring resolution before production. All detailed UX, numerical limits, commercial terms and technical implementations are defaults unless explicitly marked confirmed.

This is a complete product definition for prototype planning, not a claim that pricing, legal terms, provider contracts or app-store acceptance have been approved. The prototype must demonstrate unresolved integrations using clearly labelled simulations, never false claims of live verification or payment processing.

The product is broad in the kinds of human connection it supports. It is not a generic super-app for unrelated services. No name availability or trademark clearance is claimed. The unidentified comparator called “whisper” remains unidentified; do not assume it is Whisper, Breeze or Thursday.
