Safety & Support

Help outside Swoovr in your country, and how to report or block someone on Swoovr.

Country set for this service — change it if it’s wrong.

If you’re in immediate danger, call 000 now.

  • Triple Zero (000) Emergency
    Police, fire and ambulance. Call if you or someone else is in danger now. Call 000
  • Police Assistance Line Police (not an emergency)
    Report a crime to police when it isn’t an emergency. Call 131 444
  • 1800RESPECT Sexual assault and violence
    24/7 support for sexual assault and domestic or family violence. Call 1800 737 732
  • Lifeline Crisis support
    24/7 crisis support. Call 13 11 14
  • eSafety Commissioner Online abuse
    Report serious online abuse and image-based abuse. Website
  • ACCCE Child exploitation
    Report online child sexual exploitation. Website

These are outside services, not Swoovr. Opening one doesn’t send them anything about you or your reports.

On Swoovr

To report or block someone, open their profile or your conversation and choose Report or Block. For assault, violence, threats, stalking, coercion or child exploitation, report a serious safety incident.
