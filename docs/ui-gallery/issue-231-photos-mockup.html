Issue #231: what changed and what's still open

Static mockup. Fictional adult member (Sam, Adelaide). Every screen in the Screen menu works, and most buttons do something (add, remove, reorder by dragging, set main, delete, open a photo). Photos are drawn shapes. Nothing is saved; reload to reset.

Basic issues → mockup

  • Rules line spans the full page width in its own band. No narrow container, no early wrap.
  • Photos for each mode: pick a mode and you see only the photos in that mode, in order, main first. Each has a small ✕ to remove it from that mode. Add photos opens a picker. Drag to reorder (desktop); the viewer has Move earlier/later for keyboard and touch.
  • The "free: up to 8 photos per mode… Free 8 · Plus 16 · Premium 24" line is gone. Only a count shows ("5 of 8").
  • "Choose a main photo…" is a normal paragraph that wraps with its card.
  • All library photos merged into one Library card with filters. Each tile says where it's used and has a small Delete button.
  • Click any photo to open it large in a viewer with previous/next and actions.
  • Not approved photos stay visible to their owner, blurred, with a Not approved label. The viewer can reveal it and offers Appeal.
  • Photo decisions and appeals moved to the bottom of the page.

Private albums (larger issues)

  • 4 album slots for every member, 10 photos each. Every album photo uses a library spot; a library photo added to an album still uses only one spot.
  • Two ways in: add an existing library photo, or upload straight into an album. A photo uploaded into an album that a reviewer marks as adult is approved for albums only: it can't be put in a mode.
  • Each album has Share in modes switches. In a chat in that mode, the member can share the album; they can stop sharing at any time.
  • Library meter shows profile photos and album-only photos separately against one 60-spot total (D293).
  • DEFAULTAlbum-only photos and album covers are blurred on the owner's own screens until tapped, so they're not exposed on a shared screen.
  • DEFAULTRemoving an album-only photo from its album keeps it in the library (it still uses a spot) until deleted.

Still open (needs owner decision)

  • OPENAdult photos conflict with D178 (CONFIRMED: no explicit content anywhere in the beta; albums only for non-explicit photos, after re-reading App Store and Google Play policy). The issue would supersede it; needs an explicit decision, and the native apps may need albums-only content hidden.
  • OPEN4 albums for every tier conflicts with the tier spec (QUESTIONS.md: Free gets 1 album, Plus/Premium more).
  • OPENWhere photo appeals go. Mockup: the existing media appeals queue (a different Admin decides), with an option to ask for the photo to go into a private album instead. Zammad only if the member contacts Support.
  • OPENDoes a not-approved photo use a library spot while it can still be appealed? Mockup: yes, until deleted.
  • OPENDoes sharing an album expire, or last until the member stops sharing? Mockup: until stopped, or until either person disconnects.
