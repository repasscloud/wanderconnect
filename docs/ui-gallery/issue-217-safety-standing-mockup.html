What changed, against issue #217

Static mockup for review. Fictional adult member (Sam, Adelaide, born 14 March 1990). Only the three screens below work; other links are inert. Support service names and numbers are illustrative; the live page keeps reading them from the country safety pack.

§1 Navigation

  • Privacy & safety → /me/safety (was /me/standing).
  • New Account standing entry → /me/standing, after Privacy & safety. A dot appears next to it when something needs action.
  • /me/safety now uses the settings shell (it used a bare column, which is why it rendered narrow).
  • /safety stays the public, signed-out-capable page; its "Privacy & account safety" link now goes to /me/safety. All routes and deep links (/me/safety#serious, ?type=…&id=…) stay.

§2–3 Account standing

  • Status becomes a compact tag in the page head. The "Nothing to do" card is gone; to-dos and actions in force only render when they exist, as a prominent banner.
  • Verification is reduced to a compact summary with a Manage verification button. ID reads "Not verified" (no "optional").
  • Age shows the number from the DOB (36). DOB shows its source: Entered by you or Matches your ID. Age never claims to be verified unless the ID check matched.
  • Decisions and Your reports keep their content; each report gets a Contact support button carrying its reference.
  • "Support and complaints" block removed here (it lives on /me/support). One Need help? card with buttons.

§4, §8 Copy and links

  • Removed: page intro line; "Support requests are prioritised by urgency…" disclaimer; empty-state paragraphs for sections with nothing in them.
  • Kept (legal/safety): "We never show who reported anyone…" once, under Your reports; emergency services line.
  • Removed the self-link "Account standing and support references". "Notices and reports" is a secondary button. "Safety & Support" link replaced by the Privacy & safety page.

§5–6 Desktop layouts

  • Both pages use the full settings content width (up to 1,280px shell): history on the left, verification/help or tools on the right; single column under 720px of content width.
  • On Privacy & safety, emergency assistance spans the top and stays first on phones; the serious-incident card is first in the main column.

§7 Country default

  • Order: ?country= → the member's remembered manual choice → Home City country (GeoCity.CountryCode, e.g. Adelaide → AU) → Cloudflare (where trusted) → deployment fallback.
  • Hint reads "From your home city, Adelaide". Choosing another country never changes the Home City.
  • BUILDPlaceView carries only the country name today; expose the ISO code (or resolve it server-side in the BFF) rather than mapping names.

Labels

  • DEFAULTKeep the label "Privacy & safety" (pointing to /me/safety) with a small Privacy card (blocked people, your data, Privacy Notice), not a separate privacy page.
  • DEFAULTA remembered manual country choice outranks Home City (the member chose it deliberately).
  • OPENMerge /me/safety into /safety? Mockup keeps both: /safety = public directory, /me/safety = member tools. No duplicated forms.
  • OPENShould a New-member state hide the Decisions/Reports cards entirely (shown as one line here)?
