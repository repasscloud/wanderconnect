# Trust & Safety leadership and operations — draft role charter

Status: **DRAFT 0.1, 30 October 2026**, issue [119](https://github.com/repasscloud/wanderconnect/issues/119). CONFIRMED D220 implements the [owner governance decision on106](https://github.com/repasscloud/wanderconnect/issues/106#issuecomment-6470617112). The role structure below is DEFAULT; appointment and operational acceptance remain OPEN. Legal review57 applies. No named appointment, paid team, on-call coverage or Code status is asserted.

## Accountable appointment

Before external beta, record a named senior accountable Trust & Safety Leader, appointing authority, acceptance date, delegated authority, reporting route, alternate and conflict substitute in a restricted appointment record. The founder may initially hold the role; permission to do so is not an appointment. The leader must have authority to escalate safety risks, request resources, stop unsafe releases and require documented corrective actions. Uninvolved senior oversight must remain available when the leader or founder is implicated.

## Responsibilities and separation

| Responsibility | Accountable role / control |
| --- | --- |
| Risk, policy, staffing and release readiness | Leader maintains risk register and gives evidence-backed recommendations to company leadership; unresolved legal/operational gates remain visible. |
| Case triage and moderation | Individually authorised trained employees or qualified external specialists; named queue owner and deputy, least privilege and audited decisions. |
| Sensitive evidence, disclosure and hold release | Separately designated authorisers under police procedure; legal review and independent approval where required. Leader title alone grants no data access. |
| Security and privacy incidents | Security/legal incident lead with uninvolved substitute; coordinate with breach runbook and independent investigation. |
| Training and staff welfare | Leader maintains role-specific initial/refresher assessment records, supervision, specialist referral and workload support. |
| Oversight and external engagement | Leader maintains governance review, industry-engagement records, assessment and compliance evidence; no unverified public rating. |

Establish an urgent escalation route that does not depend solely on routine support hours. Record primary/backup duty owners, handoff, acknowledgement, failure fallback and rehearsal evidence privately. Existing police-procedure emergency preparation and internal targets remain requirements to operationally validate; they are not a public SLA or proof of staffed 24/7 monitoring. Do not activate public promises before real coverage exists.

DEFAULT capacity review: examine queue age, incident severity, urgent acknowledgement failures, case complexity, training gaps, conflicts, reviewer wellbeing and membership/incident growth at each governance review and after a material incident. Record required staffing/resources, owner, due date and interim risk control. No invented staffing ratio or guaranteed response time.

## Accountability records

Keep appointment/delegation history, training, policy changes, incident-process reviews, audits, findings and governance decisions for at least two years, longer where another applicable rule requires it (D220). This minimum applies to compliance evidence, not blanket retention of member content; minimise personal data and use restricted case references. Review governance formally at least annually and after material regulatory/operational change. Preserve separate police-procedure quarterly reviews and annual independent audit requirements.

Outstanding119: named accepted appointment and alternate, qualified staff/specialists, funded capacity, exercised urgent route, training evidence and formal review date. A completed charter does not satisfy these operational actions.
