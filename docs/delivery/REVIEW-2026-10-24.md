## Risks found in the code

1. **Home discovery is not limited to a market, so a traveller can reach locals early.** `GET /api/v1/explore` (`src/Swoovr.Api/Profiles/ProfileEndpoints.cs:87`–`112`) and `GET /api/v1/profiles/{id}` list and open any eligible member in the mode, whatever their home city. A member whose home city is Sydney therefore sees Adelaide members in Explore and can send them a request from the person page; only trip-linked requests check the market (`src/Swoovr.Api/Connections/RequestEndpoints.cs:39`–`42`). That bypasses the confirmed rules that travellers get no feed of locals and do not initiate before arrival (Q17, D60, D107, D122) and Q07 rule 2 ("discovery … geographically restricted until their respective markets are activated"). Beta enrolment keeps the exposure to invited testers today. Fix proposed as slice O ([Q24](QUESTIONS.md#review-and-next-slices-questions--24-october-2026)).
2. **No verification gate exists.** `DiscoveryEligibility.Adult` (`src/Swoovr.Domain/Discovery/ConnectionModes.cs:124`) treats the Keycloak 18+ tick (`Attested`) as enough for discovery and requests. `docs/product/04-IDENTITY-TRUST-AND-SAFETY.md:28` has a DEFAULT gate "verify adulthood and identity before live profile browsing/contact", still OPEN under Q04 ([Q26](QUESTIONS.md#review-and-next-slices-questions--24-october-2026)).
3. **Founding status can be granted without verification.** D79 lets an admin grant it until Q04 exists, but Q11 rule 21 and D63 require Photo and/or ID verification first. When ID verification lands the admin route needs a rule ([Q29](QUESTIONS.md#review-and-next-slices-questions--24-october-2026)).
4. **Admin verified has no review record.** `MemberProfile.AdminVerified` is a bool with a time (`src/Swoovr.Domain/Accounts/MemberProfile.cs:40`) and no route sets it. Q11 rule 19 requires reviewer, date, outcome, reason and revocation history. The audit log deliberately holds ids only (D70), so the reason must live in a restricted review table, not the audit.
5. **Staff and their member accounts are not linked.** Staff sign in through the staff realm; their personal Swoovr account is a members-realm account (D31, Q11 rules 29–31). Q11 rule 41 needs conflict-of-interest checks ("preventing an employee from reviewing reports concerning their own account"), which needs a staff-to-member link that does not exist yet ([Q33](QUESTIONS.md#review-and-next-slices-questions--24-october-2026)).
6. **No staff user interface.** Admin routes are API-only and the backend-for-frontend forwards only member routes (D84). Moderation by the India-based team (D62, Q07 rules 10–16) is not practical through raw API calls ([Q35](QUESTIONS.md#review-and-next-slices-questions--24-october-2026)).
7. **Stripe webhooks need a public HTTPS endpoint.** Local development needs Stripe CLI forwarding and a webhook signing secret from the owner's Stripe account; none is configured ([Q25](QUESTIONS.md#review-and-next-slices-questions--24-october-2026)).
8. **Known test flakes:** `tests/auth-harness/session.test.mjs` "in-flight refresh" (400 vs 303) and one undiagnosed `tests/auth/members.browser.cjs` failure in slice M; the Honolulu case in `tests/travel/travel.browser.cjs` runs only at certain hours. Not product risks, but they weaken evidence.

## Conflicts between documents (need an owner answer)

1. **Intentions as exclusions.** `01-PRODUCT-BRIEF.md:17` (CONFIRMED) and `02-BEHAVIOUR-AND-RULES.md:9`–`17` require "require and exclude intentions independently", and acceptance criteria 1–2 (`06-TECHNICAL…:51`–`52`) hide an LTR+FWB candidate from someone excluding FWB. Q16 rule 10 / D106 say chips "never exclude anyone from discovery on their own". Both can hold if exclusion is a viewer's filter rather than a property of the chip, but the docs don't say so ([Q39](QUESTIONS.md#review-and-next-slices-questions--24-october-2026)). The intention vocabulary at `02-BEHAVIOUR…:5` is also superseded by the forty chips (D106).
2. **Navigation.** `01-PRODUCT-BRIEF.md:48` and `03-SCREENS-AND-DESIGN.md:15` say the tabs are Explore, Connections, Chats, Me. The app has Me, Profile, Explore, Travel (D123) and the web tab bar has Explore, Connections, Chats (prototype) and Me ([Q40](QUESTIONS.md#review-and-next-slices-questions--24-october-2026)).
3. **Visitor choices.** `02-BEHAVIOUR-AND-RULES.md:47` lets locals choose current visitors, upcoming visitors, both or neither, and makes future-visit publication opt-in. The built feed (D122) shows every visible trip to every local with no such choice. Trip visibility is already opt-in per trip, so only the local-side choice is missing; folded into the filters question ([Q36](QUESTIONS.md#review-and-next-slices-questions--24-october-2026)).
4. **Verification gate.** `04-IDENTITY…:28` (DEFAULT, gate before browsing/contact) against the Free-tier matrix (verification free, no gate stated) and the beta's open discovery today ([Q26](QUESTIONS.md#review-and-next-slices-questions--24-october-2026)).
