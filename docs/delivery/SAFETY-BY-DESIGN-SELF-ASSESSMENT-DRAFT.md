## Assessment record

Record assessment ID/version, assessed build/commit, environments and markets, assessor, accountable leader, date, evidence cutoff, independent/legal reviewers, review outcome and next review. All are **OPEN** until recorded. Consult current authoritative Safety by Design and applicable Code/standards material during the real assessment and record source/version/date; this template does not independently verify current legal applicability.

For each scenario record affected people/context, plausible misuse, severity/likelihood rationale, preventive control, detection, response, remedy, residual risk, evidence reference, limitations, accountable action owner and due date. Consider risks across dating, friendship, casual and business modes without exposing hidden mode information.

## Evidence and outcome rules

Classify each control as fulfilled, partially fulfilled, outstanding or justified not applicable, with environment and evidence date. A document or local synthetic test alone cannot establish hosted operation, real provider acceptance, training completion or monitoring coverage. Record missing/contradictory evidence explicitly. Keep sensitive case material outside the repository and use restricted references.

The leader records residual risks, required remediation, release consequence, legal/independent review and next reassessment. Link findings to the [compliance register](CODE-COMPLIANCE-REGISTER-DRAFT.md). No Code tier or public verified rating is assigned here; formal participation and external verification must precede any such claim. Reassess at least annually and after material regulatory/operational change; complete identified launch actions before representing readiness.
