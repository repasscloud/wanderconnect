## Record custody and review

CONFIRMED D220: retain compliance evidence for at least two years, longer where another applicable rule requires it; review governance at least annually and after material regulatory or operational change. DEFAULT: version this non-sensitive register and keep appointment/training/audit/correspondence evidence in an access-controlled operational store with an owner, retention/review dates and change history. Store minimal case references here, no private messages, identities, police material or production exports. Member evidence follows its separate necessity/hold/retention rules; the compliance minimum does not justify copying it into the register.

Before Code application or any public rating, the leader must reconcile every applicable requirement with current evidence, have legal review confirm obligations, identify outstanding actions and complete the required external process. Drafting this register does not close119 or waive other launch gates.
