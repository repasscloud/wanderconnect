# Code and governance compliance register — draft

Status: **DRAFT 0.2 — working register, 2 November 2026**, issue [119](https://github.com/repasscloud/wanderconnect/issues/119), CONFIRMED D215/D220. **No obligation is recorded as fulfilled.** Swoovr intends to seek Code participation subject to legal and ongoing operational readiness; it is not represented as a signatory, externally verified or compliant. Legal review ([57](https://github.com/repasscloud/wanderconnect/issues/57)) remains required. DRAFT 0.2 adds the mandatory statutory obligations that apply whether or not Swoovr signs the voluntary Code.

Status vocabulary: **Fulfilled** requires dated evidence for the relevant environment and an authorised reviewer. **Partially fulfilled** identifies evidence and outstanding scope. **Outstanding** lacks necessary implementation or operational evidence. **Not applicable** requires recorded justification and review. The dated repository [gap analysis](ONLINE-DATING-CODE.md) is a starting point, not a verified assessment.

## Register entry requirements

Each real register entry additionally needs a control ID, source and version, assessed commit and environment, evidence date and restricted reference, named accountable owner, reviewer, residual risk, action and due date, verification outcome and next review. Role names above are responsibility proposals; they do not identify appointed people. New evidence must update status explicitly rather than silently treating an open issue as complete.

## Record custody and review

CONFIRMED D220: retain compliance evidence for at least two years, longer where another applicable rule requires it, and review governance at least annually and after material regulatory or operational change. DEFAULT: version this non-sensitive register and keep appointment, training, audit and correspondence evidence in an access-controlled operational store with an owner, retention and review dates, and change history. Store only minimal case references here: no private messages, identities, police material or production exports. Member evidence follows its separate necessity, hold and retention rules; the compliance minimum does not justify copying it into the register.

Before any Code application or public rating, the leader must reconcile every applicable requirement with current evidence, have legal review confirm obligations, identify outstanding actions and complete the required external process. Drafting this register does not close 119 or waive other launch gates.
