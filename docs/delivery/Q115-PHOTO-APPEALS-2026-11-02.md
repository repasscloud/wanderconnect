# Q115 — Private photo decisions and independent appeals

CONFIRMED D298 ([owner answer](https://github.com/repasscloud/wanderconnect/issues/160#issuecomment-6572660549), 2 November 2026) requires in-app rejection/outcome notices, one appeal within 30 days, a different Admin's final decision, no appeal/detailed reason for specialist or known-illegal media, private rejected-file retention for 30 days then hold-guarded deletion, and reasoned Admin reconsideration with fresh screening. Sanction appeals remain separate.

## Implementation

Immutable `MediaDecisionRecord` rows retain prior decisions; member wording derives from a fixed safe code mapping, never reviewer notes, classifier scores, protected evidence or staff identity. Rejection and final outcome notices are durable in-app `PhotoDecision` records with unique per-decision dedupe keys. They do not dispatch email or push. Web/native photo pages show safe decisions and eligible appeal forms; the notification centre links to Your photos.

Only the latest operative ordinary rejection can be appealed. The member must own it, have a retained live rejected source and remain within its 30-day decision window. Submission rechecks the current clock/state/latest decision under account-lifecycle then media authority. A unique decision index prevents duplicate appeals. A different, non-conflicted Admin resolves it; Support cannot. Final outcomes cannot be replayed or bypassed by generic reconsideration. Rejected images remain hidden throughout the appeal.

The generic reconsideration route and pipeline both recheck appeal finality under the same authority used by submission/resolution. Fresh screening is mandatory. The pipeline refreshes TimeProvider after authority waits and after the remote screen, so stale caller timestamps cannot extend safety acknowledgement or source retention. Failed/uncertain/incomplete screening cannot approve. A blocked fresh result commits a hidden specialist source/preservation intent and closes the appeal once as generic `unavailable`; it does not reveal why. The existing preservation worker owns subsequent specialist recovery.

A clear fresh result restores the library item only, without silently restoring former mode placements. It retains the same private object key to avoid source loss between object-storage writes and database commit. Appeal completion, decision history and the final member notice commit together. Generic reconsideration records its reason separately from coded approval, and refuses any existing appeal for that photo.

New rejections set `RejectedRetainUntil` to the actual rejection time plus 30 days. Physical deletion refuses earlier removal and then uses the existing safety-retention/live-hold checks; expired missing sources cannot be approved. Existing historical records receive no invented clock or appealability. Member exports include their own appeal text/outcomes but omit private reviewer notes. Legal holds capture decision/appeal metadata before final account deletion removes ordinary rows; existing evidence custody/retention rules govern those held copies.

## Issue 232 — appeals against your own decision stay visible (7 November 2026)

[#232](https://github.com/repasscloud/wanderconnect/issues/232) (UAT): a member's appeal looked lost because the only Admin was the one who made the rejection. D298 sends it to a different Admin, so the queue, the Appeals tab count and the Overview tile all showed 0, with nothing to say why. The rule is unchanged: the deciding Admin still cannot list, view or decide the appeal.

DEFAULT: `PhotoWorkload.AwaitingOtherAdmin` and `PhotoAppealPage.AwaitingOtherAdmin` count undecided appeals against this Admin's own decision, using the same eligibility as the queue, so excluded or linked members and the Admin's own media are never counted. The Appeals tab shows them as a separate dashed badge, the Overview "media appeals" tile adds "N appeals waiting for another Admin", and the appeals page says "No appeals for you to decide" with the reason, not "No appeals waiting". A single-Admin environment still needs a second Admin account to decide the appeal.

Validation: `PhotoReviewPersistenceChecks` adds three PostgreSQL checks (deciding Admin counted and not listed, a different Admin listed and not counted, excluded Admin not counted). `tests/media/review-workflows.browser.cjs` asserts the workload and appeal-page counts before and after the independent decision; that suite needs the full local stack and was not run for this change.

Observed 7 November 2026: `./scripts/check.sh` exit 0 with `SWOOVR_TEST_DATABASE` pointing at a disposable PostgreSQL 17.11 container (728 persistence cases PASS, including the three above). The tab badge, empty state, results-head note and Overview tile were rendered with the real `themes.css`/`admin.css` in headless Chrome (dark, desktop width); that showed the note made the appeals tile taller than its row, so `.tiles a` now fills the row height. Not observed: the live admin pages against a running stack, phone width, or assistive technology.
