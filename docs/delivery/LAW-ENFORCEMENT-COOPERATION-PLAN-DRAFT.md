# Law-enforcement cooperation and industry engagement plan — draft

Status: **DRAFT 0.1, 30 October 2026**, issue [119](https://github.com/repasscloud/wanderconnect/issues/119), under CONFIRMED D220. Planning only; no outreach, agreement, verified contact, protocol adoption or signatory status is claimed. Legal57 and operational review remain required. The [police-contact procedure](POLICE-CONTACT-PROCEDURE.md) governs actual requests and disclosures.

## Readiness sequence

1. Leader designates named authority-contact owner and deputy, authorised approvers and counsel. Confirm ownership, secure access, real monitoring and backup escalation for the policy's lawenforcement@swoovr.app address and public law-enforcement page; a configured/public link is not proof of delivery or staffing. Record an actual controlled intake/acknowledgement rehearsal without member data.
2. Independently verify current official contact points for each Australian policing jurisdiction and ANZPAA. Record official source, verification date, verifier and secure contact route. Do not treat an address supplied in an incoming request as verification.
3. Obtain and legally assess the current ANZPAA information-sharing protocol, participation eligibility, permitted uses, security/privacy duties, reporting and preservation obligations, and how it interacts with Swoovr's existing procedure. Record advice and whether adoption is appropriate; investigation is not adoption.
4. Prepare individually reviewed invitations to Australian police agencies concerning appropriate cooperation/MOUs, or document the legally approved protocol route. No invitation is sent by this draft. Track replies, scope, authorised signatories, execution dates and outstanding actions. Do not describe an unsigned draft or silence as an executed agreement.
5. Rehearse standard, urgent, imminent-threat, foreign-authority and fraudulent-request cases. Verify independent callback, legal basis, narrow scope, separate preservation/disclosure approval, secure transfer, notification decision and auditable closure. No personal information is disclosed merely because someone claims law-enforcement status.
6. Review before beta/public launch and following material legal, protocol or operational changes. Escalate gaps to the leader; unrelated development may continue under D220.
