## On receiving a report or detecting harm

1. Open the assigned case using your individual authorised account. Check subject/reporter and retained personal-account conflicts before reading evidence or acting. Declare any personal relationship or staff implication and transfer to an uninvolved reviewer. Do not use shared accounts or infer permissions from training, email or a badge.
2. Triage available facts for immediate danger, threats, stalking, sexual assault, exploitation, extortion, suspected minors, suspected CSAM, account compromise and evidence loss. Record receipt/triage times, known facts, uncertainty, priority and owner. Prioritise serious risks and use the designated urgent escalation route independent of routine support hours. Record acknowledgement and escalate to the backup when the primary is unavailable; do not assume an email was read. Real roster, acknowledgement and fallback rehearsal are launch prerequisites, not evidence supplied by this draft.
3. Give supportive, neutral information: acknowledge the report, explain that the person need not contact the alleged offender, and offer the country-appropriate Safety & Support pack. Ask only for relevant information; do not demand a police report or unnecessary intimate detail. Do not promise an outcome, recovery, confidentiality without exceptions or a response time. Direct immediate danger to local emergency services; Swoovr cannot dispatch responders or guarantee monitoring.
4. Preserve only relevant existing evidence through approved restricted tools. Record IDs, profile version/context, source, time range and preservation justification. Request an authorised scoped hold where needed; ordinary removal and safety restrictions may continue without erasing preserved originals. Do not download, forward or repeatedly view suspected CSAM: seal access and refer to trained authorised personnel using the specialist legal process. Never put member content in ordinary logs, personal email/cloud storage or unauthorised AI services.
5. Investigate fairly within assigned scope. Separate allegations, observed evidence, automated signals and conclusions. Consider context and possible mistakes; phone/IP/image reuse alone is not proof of identity or abuse. Uncertain automated results require appropriate human review; no automatic permanent ban solely for image reuse or negative feedback. Do not open unrelated identities, modes or conversations to satisfy curiosity.
6. Record the applicable policy basis, evidence references, risk rationale, authorised action and reviewer. Use the least intrusive effective measure: warning or limited restriction for lesser harm; suspension where justified; termination for serious breaches following applicable authority. All-mode sanctions and block protection remain enforced. Never quietly reverse a ban, release an identifier or reinstate an account through an unrelated action. Staff-affiliated cases require independent handling.
7. Communicate the general outcome where safe and lawful, with the available reference and appeal/help route. Do not reveal the reporter, another person's identity, protected evidence or confidential police work. Outcome notifications, handling complaints and Code complaints remain separate implementation work under117; record any delivery gap for follow-up rather than claiming a notice was sent.
8. Close only after checking actions, required communications, continuing holds, appeal/review dates and assigned follow-ups. Closure does not release holds or authorise disposal. Retained review evidence follows the current schedule; unknown preservation membership fails closed. Record handoff and unresolved risks explicitly.

## Specialist escalation

| Trigger | Route and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Immediate danger / serious violence or sexual harm | Urgent Trust & Safety lead and authorised backup; apply police procedure for any emergency disclosure. Preserve and disclose are separate decisions. |
| Suspected CSAM / child exploitation | Trained specialist and legal lead; no informal circulation, unapproved external upload or independent age inference from a classifier. |
| Police or regulator request | Designated authority intake; independently verify requester, legal basis, scope and approver before disclosure. Claiming to be police is insufficient. |
| Staff implication / reviewer conflict | Uninvolved senior representative, external counsel where warranted; restrict implicated access. |
| Evidence leak / compromised access | Security incident lead via breach runbook; contain access and preserve forensic records. |
| Disputed action / complaint about handling | Uninvolved reviewer using approved appeal/support mechanisms; no retaliation or promise of unavailable complaint tooling. |

## Training record and assessment template

Before access: record staff identifier, role/scope, trainer, training date, policy versions, scenario results, remediation, assessor approval and access-review date in restricted operational records. Training alone never grants access. Cover privacy, least privilege, conflicts, trauma-informed communication, coercive control/sexual misconduct, minors/CSAM, automation limits, evidence integrity, police verification, retention and breach response. Provide initial and annual refresher training and annual permission review under the police procedure; repeat after material changes or incidents. Arrange workload support and access to qualified specialists; record staffing gaps.

DEFAULT rehearsal cases: threat followed by unsend; sexual-assault report after block; apparent recycled phone match; staff-linked reporter; sealed known-content image; unverified police email; overlapping hold at closure; and evidence disclosed to the wrong recipient. For each record expected action, actual action, evidence, errors, remediation and independent sign-off. Use fictional adults and synthetic non-abusive fixtures only. No rehearsal is recorded as completed here.
