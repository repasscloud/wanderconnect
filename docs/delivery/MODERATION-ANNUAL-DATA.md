## Recorded data and limits

New terminations, individual text removals and terminal image refusals commit a minimal immutable action receipt in the same transaction as their source. It contains opaque restricted provenance, action time, controlled reviewed policy basis, detection/enforcement mechanism, initiative, automation definition and server-selected dataset. No content, contact, location coordinates or staff free-text reason is copied. For terminations, country is the member's declared home country at action, reduced to AU/other/unknown; it is not verified residence. Historical sources are never assigned today's profile country or inferred policy basis. Receipt replay preserves its original values.

Admin explicitly selects a controlled reviewed basis; a member-report or text-candidate detection selection must reference an actual source in that case. Staff-review is an explicit attestation. Unselected or unavailable evidence remains unknown. Image screening has an unversioned definition until its provider/version is established; human handling of uncertain images is distinguished from automatic refusal. A moderation decision is not a unique complaint or content item.

Anonymous UTC daily totals record completed inspections, incomplete text-history inspections, created review candidates and failed processing attempts by two fixed definitions and dataset. They store no account/source identifiers. Completion and its source checkpoint commit together; rollback/replay cannot count an additional completed transition. Processing attempts are not independently labelled classifier mistakes or false-positive/negative rates. No eligible-write denominator, measured accuracy or completed training is asserted.

Reporting:Dataset defaults to local-synthetic in Development and unknown elsewhere. Explicit production is permitted only in Production, never UAT. This does not make local fixtures production evidence. The annual query includes production-labelled receipts and separately discloses retained legacy gaps; disposed history and unknown historical country cannot be reconstructed.

## Access and disclosure

Only current staff Admin authority can read the fixed completed UTC year or download aggregate JSON. Saving an internal revision additionally requires fresh authentication; the SSR form requires antiforgery and explicit confirmation. All responses use no-store. No member BFF route, per-person filters, source IDs or private reasons are exposed.

AU terminations count distinct accounts, with separately non-additive reviewed bases. Content decisions group detection/enforcement; initiative and automation are disclosed separately. If any measured numeric cell is 1–4, all numeric cells are suppressed, including complementary totals; first recording month is hidden too. Unknown/unmeasured values remain null. Zero means no rows in the recorded subset, not no events in the service. Coverage remains explicitly incomplete.

Saved aggregate revisions are immutable and identical content reuses the prior revision. Saving does not publish. No operator can upload arbitrary report text through this API. Corrected recorded data creates a later internal revision while retained source windows permit it.

## Retention and preservation

DEFAULT action receipts and anonymous daily rows expire after 36 calendar months. Before first expiry, the worker archives the completed year's aggregate internally; saved aggregate-only revisions survive source expiry. Expired years cannot be manually recomputed from partial surviving history. Holds, unresolved coverage and retained evidence copies conservatively prevent personal receipt erasure. Evidence capture includes applicable receipts; the ordinary export includes only the member's own country-at-action declaration and date/action, without internal detection/basis/case/source metadata.

Database guards reject receipt rewrites, premature disposal, missing source provenance and snapshot mutation. They supplement application authority; migration/database owners remain privileged. Proposed periods and publication policy remain subject to legal review #57. Actual production collection, training evidence, report review/publication and eSafety submission require owner operations.
