Swoovr is an adult connection service. You must be at least 18 to use it, legally able to agree to these terms, and not prohibited from using it by law or by an earlier Swoovr decision to permanently close your account (unless we have reinstated it). One person may have one account. Venue partner and staff accounts are covered by separate agreements.

### 3. Your account

Give accurate account information, keep it up to date, and use only accounts you are authorised to control. Your mobile number and email address must be yours and verified. Protect your sign-in details, don't share your account, and tell support at once if your account may be compromised. You are responsible for activity on your account that you could reasonably have prevented.

### 4. Connecting and meeting safely

You control whether to connect, continue a conversation or meet. Respect another person's refusal, withdrawal of consent or decision to stop contact. Verification badges describe particular checks; they do not guarantee a person's identity, intentions, conduct or safety, and Swoovr does not run criminal background checks. Swoovr cannot guarantee a connection, meeting, relationship or protection from harm. Use our safety advice and tools, meet in public, and arrange your own travel. Swoovr is not an emergency dispatch service; if you face immediate danger, contact your local emergency service (000 in Australia) directly.

### 5. Your content

You keep ownership of the profile details, photos, messages and other content you post. You give us a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide licence to host, store, copy, adapt (for example resize or remove hidden photo data), display and transmit that content only as needed to operate Swoovr according to your settings, to moderate and keep Swoovr safe, and to comply with the law. The licence ends when your content is deleted, except for copies we must keep under the privacy notice, such as preserved evidence. We don't use your private messages, photos or identity information for advertising or to train AI models. Only post content you have the right and permission to share, and never another person's private information or intimate images without their consent.

### 6. Using Swoovr lawfully

Use Swoovr lawfully and follow the community guidelines in every mode, profile, message, image and interaction connected with the service. Don't misuse Swoovr: don't scrape, copy or harvest other members' information, interfere with or probe our systems, use automated tools or fake accounts, sell or advertise to members without our written permission, or evade a block, restriction or ban. Swoovr's software, design and brand belong to us or our licensors; you may use them only to use Swoovr for yourself.

### 7. Paid features

Swoovr's safety features, reporting, blocking and support are free for everyone. If we offer paid features, we will show the price, what is included, billing period, renewal and cancellation terms before you buy. Purchases made through Apple or Google are billed and cancelled through that store under its terms; purchases on our website are billed through our payment provider. You can cancel a renewing purchase at any time; it stops at the end of the current paid period. Refunds are available where the Australian Consumer Law requires them, and otherwise as explained at the time of purchase. Complimentary or beta access is free and is never charged automatically.

### 8. Reports, moderation and appeals

You can report conduct or content and block an account through the in-app controls, including after blocking or ending a conversation. We may assess reports, preserve relevant evidence and take proportionate action, such as removing content, issuing a warning, restricting features, suspending or permanently closing an account, or referring a matter to police. Serious violations, including any child sexual exploitation, may lead to immediate permanent closure. We assess available evidence fairly; a report is an allegation, not proof. Where safe and lawful, we tell you about an action on your account and why, without exposing another person's private information or a protected investigation. You can appeal an action once within 30 days through Account standing; someone other than the original decision-maker reviews it.

### 9. Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time using the account controls. Deletion hides your account at once and becomes final after 30 days, or straight away if you choose. We may suspend or close your account if you seriously or repeatedly breach these terms or the community guidelines, if the law requires it, or if it is reasonably necessary to protect members or others from harm. We will tell you why where it is safe and lawful to do so. If we stop offering Swoovr, we will give you reasonable notice and refund the unused part of any prepaid paid feature. Deletion, unsending and blocking do not necessarily erase evidence already preserved for a lawful safety review or legal hold.

### 10. Privacy

Our privacy notice explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and your rights to access and correct it. We handle your information as described there. We cannot recover information that has already been lawfully deleted.

If a change materially affects you, we will tell you in the app at least 14 days before it takes effect, unless a shorter time is needed for legal or safety reasons, and we will ask for your explicit acceptance where required. If you don't agree to a change, you can delete your account before it takes effect, and we will refund the unused part of any prepaid paid feature.

### 13. Help, complaints and disputes

For help, use Swoovr support (<https://swoovr.com/support>) or <support@swoovr.com> and include your reference if one is shown. You can also complain to us about how we handled a report or about our compliance with the online dating industry code through the same channels; someone not involved in the original decision will review it. Intake availability does not mean continuous human monitoring or a promised response time. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the eSafety Commissioner (esafety.gov.au) about online harm, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au) about privacy, or your state or territory consumer protection agency. These terms are governed by the law in force in South Australia, and the courts of South Australia and the courts that can hear appeals from them have non-exclusive jurisdiction. ## Proposed community guidelines

- Adults only. No accounts operated by or for people under 18, no child sexual abuse or exploitation material, no sexual content involving minors and no grooming. Report suspected underage use; do not investigate it yourself.
- No illegal content or conduct, sexual assault, sexual harassment, sexual coercion, exploitation, stalking, threats, violence or encouragement of harm.
- Consent comes first. No unsolicited sexual content. Consent must be freely given, specific and ongoing; a profile preference, match or earlier conversation does not establish consent to another act or image. Stop when someone says no or stops replying.
- No non-consensual intimate images, threats to share them, image-based abuse, sexual extortion, or manipulated intimate material shared without consent.
- Be yourself. No impersonation, fake or duplicate accounts, deceptive synthetic profile imagery, or misleading information about your age, identity or relationship status where it matters to the other person.
- No fraud, scams, romance scams, requests for money, gifts, investments or financial details, spam, bulk unsolicited outreach, malware or malicious links.
- No hate, discriminatory abuse or targeted harassment based on race, ethnicity, religion, disability, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, age or any other attribute.
- Respect privacy. No doxxing, sharing another person's private information, contact details, location or screenshots of private conversations to harass or expose them, and never reveal another person's dating activity to their professional contacts.
- Respect boundaries. No retaliation against reporters, and no attempts to bypass a block, ban, restriction or another person's privacy choices, including through another mode or account.
- No public nudity or explicit sexual profile content, paid sexual services, escort advertising or exploitative solicitation. Do not infer that a private-content feature authorises prohibited material; separately approved feature and recipient-consent rules apply.
- No commercial use. Don't advertise, recruit or sell to members, or use Swoovr for multi-level marketing, without our written permission.
- Meet safely and honestly. Don't pressure anyone to meet, change plans or go somewhere private, don't encourage dangerous behaviour, and treat people respectfully if you decide not to continue.

These guidelines apply to everyone on Swoovr, in every mode, profile, photo, message and meeting arranged through Swoovr. Breaking them can lead to content removal, restrictions, suspension or permanent account closure, and serious matters may be referred to police.

Report concerns using Swoovr's controls; you do not need to confront the person first. Give the information you reasonably have, distinguish facts from uncertainty, and do not knowingly fabricate reports. A report made in good faith is not misconduct merely because evidence is inconclusive.

## Legal sign-off checklist (issue 57)

The text above has been drafted to avoid terms likely to be unfair under the Australian Consumer Law unfair contract terms regime: no broad liability exclusions, no consumer indemnity, no unilateral variation without notice and an exit with refund, no termination for convenience without notice and refund, and an express consumer-guarantee statement. 1. Operator particulars: CONFIRMED by the owner on 2 November 2026 (RePass Cloud Pty Ltd, ABN 74 642 243 801, ACN 642 243 801, Findon SA 5023, <hello@repasscloud.com>).
2. Governing law and forum: CONFIRMED by the owner on 2 November 2026: South Australia, subject to mandatory local law (ACL, GDPR, CCPA and similar). Counsel to confirm the wording for members outside Australia.
3. Content licence scope (section 5) and the commitment not to use member content for advertising or AI training (policy §15).
4. Paid features (section 7): Plus pricing, app-store and Stripe billing, refunds and auto-renewal disclosures before any live sale (D61, D137, D151).
5. Enforcement, suspension and appeal wording (sections 8–9) against D133, D135, D160 and the moderator playbook.
6. Change notice period (section 12): 14 days is a DEFAULT.
7. Eligibility (section 2): whether to add a criminal-history condition. Not included, because Swoovr cannot verify it and spent-conviction laws apply; owner decision required.
8. Consistency with privacy notice Draft 0.17, the retention schedule and the private-content/app-store review.
