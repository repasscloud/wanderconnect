Swoovr colour system

Design concept · in development · starting in Adelaide · Adults 18+

Black and white first.
Colour is your choice.

Swoovr ships in Standard: off-black, off-white, clean and crisp. Each person can switch on one of eight schemes in Settings → Appearance, in Light, Dark or System mode. This page applies whatever you pick, so it is the live reference for the app and all marketing.

Appearance schemes

Eight schemes × light/dark = 16 resolved themes. Stable IDs are shown in brackets; persist those, never names or hex values.

Tokens

Ten semantic tokens, same names in every scheme. Click any value to copy it. Ratios are WCAG contrast for the pair that must stay readable (4.5:1 minimum for normal text).

Marketing palette

Rules

Standard is the brand

Default is Standard + System. Marketing leads with black and white; colour is a second view, never the first impression.

A preference, not an identity

A scheme changes only that person's screens. It never recolours their profile for others, shows in chat, or says anything about who they are.

Use the pairs

Text on bg/surface, onPrimary on primary, softText on soft. Never hard-code white on every primary: Standard dark, Lounge dark and Trans use dark labels.

Accent is decoration

Accent and border are not checked as text colours. Use them for lines, dots and highlights, not body copy.

Badges never change

Photo-verified blue, ID-verified teal, founding gold, staff ruby and bot silver are the same in all 16 themes.

Flags with care

Pride, Trans and Union Jack use screen palettes of real flags. Keep stripe order, don't distort them, and don't imply partnership with any organisation.
